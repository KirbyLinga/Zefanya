# Zefanya Rider and Transport Courier Rules

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Coverage and worker status
Rules apply to authorized Riders and Transport Couriers acting for approved Logistics Providers. These operational rules do not establish or negate employment status or waive mandatory wages, deductions safeguards, benefits, road-safety or other rights under Philippine law. The provider-specific lawful engagement terms remain relevant.

## 2. Authorization and accounts
Use only your own approved credentials and assigned jobs/trips. Do not hand your account to another person. Report loss of device, suspension, accident or compromised credentials promptly. Offline status does not automatically transfer assigned physical custody.

## 3. Rider pickup and station handover
Verify the assigned parcel and actual handover before confirming pickup. Follow station/barangay/provider constraints, parcel tracking verification and assigned weight/eligibility requirements. Complete the next physical transfer only with verified receiving-station acknowledgement.

## 4. Rider delivery and COD
Use genuine proof photo, real recipient name/relationship acknowledgement, the correct delivery result and exact COD confirmation. Do not fabricate a delivery, photograph unrelated private information or divert COD. Remit funds through provider procedures; the original obligation and counted cash must be separately preserved. A shortage requires review and the worker's opportunity to explain; no automatic earnings deduction follows solely from a discrepancy.

## 5. Failed delivery
Record truthful reason and notes. A failed attempt does not mean Logistics approved a return. Keep parcel secure and follow authorized station handback, retry or return decisions. Do not self-approve custody changes.

## 6. Transport Courier duties
Carry only authorized manifests/trips, respect seals and transfer instructions, record actual events, and report missing/damaged parcels immediately. Arrival does not mean receiving hub accepted custody. Each parcel requires applicable verification, including in partial receipts.

## 7. Incidents and recovery
If a worker becomes unavailable, Logistics assigns a recovery owner; custody remains with the last verified holder until a documented physical handover. No fabricated scan, forced false receipt or unaudited reassignment. Cooperate with proportionate investigations and preserve evidence.

## 8. Privacy and safety
Use address/contact details solely for assigned jobs. Do not post delivery evidence publicly, send customer information to personal groups, request passwords or record unnecessary location/ID information. Comply with lawful road and workplace safety requirements; report unsafe conditions.

## 9. Earnings, corrections and disputes
Applicable rates are disclosed by the responsible Provider and reflected in authorized rate snapshots. Payment is verified and recorded; incorrect Courier payout references and failed transfers are corrected through an audited process. Disputes must be reviewed fairly; no clause authorizes unlawful deductions.

## 10. Violations and appeals
Serious fraud, deliberate theft, identity misuse and falsified proof may lead to lawful restrictions and investigation. Allegations are not proof. Workers may provide explanations and seek available review channels.
