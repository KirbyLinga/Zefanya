# Zefanya Internal Administrator, Privacy and Finance SOP

**Classification: Internal — Not for Public Policy Pages** | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose and segregation
Admin and authorized staff are bound by least privilege, confidential handling, separation of duties and auditable decisions. Admin oversees marketplace enforcement and escalated disputes; it does **not** perform ordinary Logistics operations or substitute for verified parcel custody.

## 2. Restricted information
Protect user ID documents, addresses, private messages, parcel evidence, credential/security records, financial records, bank/payment references, security logs and dispute files. Access only for assigned legitimate purpose. Use individual authorized identities; where the Logistics account is shared at company level and cannot reliably identify staff, require a recorded name and track the authenticated provider separately rather than falsely asserting authenticated person-level attribution.

## 3. Financial controls
No refund record without eligible funded payment and authorized decision. Distinguish refund obligation from externally paid refund. COD counted cash vs original debt is immutable with append-only review/recovery audit. No automatic Rider wage deduction. Payout correction preserves original evidence and requires authorized review. Seller debt collection/write-off beyond future payout offset needs separate business approval.

## 4. Custody and incidents
Admin review does not create physical handoff. Logistics handles parcel recovery with named owner and verified station receipt before reassignment. Admin assesses escalated non-receipt/slow-hub events and remediation; review must not invent delivery, payment or transfer events.

## 5. Privacy complaints and incidents
Route privacy requests to designated privacy staff; verify identity; document access/correction/deletion decisions and lawful retention exceptions. Escalate suspected breach immediately, preserve evidence, apply risk assessment and legal reporting deadlines including notification within 72 hours where required. Document lawfully permitted postponements, if any.

## 6. Security hygiene
Restrict exports and external file sharing; store credentials only in approved secure channels; log material administrative actions; no production tests with destructive schemas; no live bank transfers without authorized financial workflow.

## 7. Training and version control
Require role-specific training and confidentiality acknowledgement. Archive policy versions and agreements; log material decision date, reviewer and case references. Periodically review access grants and vendor/Logistics data sharing.
