# Zefanya Seller Agreement

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Eligibility and merchant identity
Sellers must give truthful store, owner/representative, business contact, verification, and registration information where legally applicable. Their storefront and transaction disclosures must show legally required merchant identity and customer contact information. Approval is conditional on ongoing compliance.

## 2. Listings and lawful goods
Sellers are responsible for authorized, genuine, safe, adequately described products; pricing, inventory, product condition, warnings, warranty and applicable permits. Counterfeit, deceptive and prohibited goods are forbidden. Listing removal and enforcement may occur with documented reasons and appropriate review.

## 3. Fulfillment and handover
Sellers prepare goods, package them safely and mark Ready for Pickup only when physically ready. Logistics controls authorized Rider assignment and custody transitions. Seller must verify and document real physical handover; must not claim pickup prematurely or personally change downstream custody status.

## 4. Prices, promotions and fees
The applicable platform commission rate is disclosed in [INSERT APPROVED SELLER RATE SCHEDULE]. The current intended fee base is item subtotal after Seller-funded discounts, excluding shipping and platform-funded vouchers; final implementation must be validated before publication. Seller-funded promotions need explicit funding and validity conditions. Rates are versioned; later changes do not rewrite completed order snapshots.

## 5. Earnings, COD and payouts
Seller proceeds are distinct from cash received by Riders or Logistics. Payouts follow verified order/settlement records and eligible deductions. Requests may require approval and verifiable actual disbursement; Zefanya will not record unpaid funds as paid. A completed payout is immutable history. Valid returns may create documented Seller debt or recoverable obligations against future lawful payouts. Sellers can contest incorrect financial assessments.

## 6. Consumer protection and returns
Sellers must handle genuine defective/wrong/damaged/incomplete complaints and cooperate with lawful refunds/replacements, including claims after order completion. Seven-day ordinary online reporting does not cancel statutory warranty rights. Approved physical return, verified Seller receipt, refund owed/paid, and stock restoration are distinct events. Stock is not restored until the authorized physical receipt condition is met.

## 7. Privacy and customer information
Sellers may process customer information only to fulfill their own orders, support legitimate disputes, and comply with legal duties. No resale, off-platform marketing without lawful basis, harassment, unnecessary copying or sharing of addresses, proof images or messages. Breach suspicions must be reported promptly. Sellers may be independent personal information controllers for their own processing; applicable privacy/data-sharing responsibilities are separately documented.

## 8. Fraud and enforcement
Misrepresentation, unauthorized use of orders or delivery records, fake transactions, evidence manipulation and attempts to conceal debt are prohibited. Restrictions are proportionate, documented and subject to appropriate review. Closing or suspending an account does not settle verified obligations or erase legally retained records.

## 9. Outstanding debt and unresolved cases
Existing approved debt offset rules may apply to subsequent eligible payouts. Automatic collection from unrelated accounts, off-platform seizure, compromise or write-off is **not authorized by these terms without a separately approved lawful agreement/process**. Parties retain legal dispute rights.
