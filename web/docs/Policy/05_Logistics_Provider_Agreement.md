# Zefanya Logistics Provider Agreement

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Approval and service coverage
Participating Providers must provide accurate legal/business details and relevant licenses/permits. Zefanya's agreed routing model requires provider-specific provincial authority and validated barangay-level coverage, plus approved directional transport connections where necessary. Providers must not accept unsupported routes as confirmed capacity.

## 2. Physical custody and parcel integrity
A Provider assumes custody only following the authorized verified receipt, and transfers custody only through required verified handover. Assignment, arrival, manifest registration, status edit or an incident alone is not proof of physical receipt. Partial manifest receipt leaves missing parcels with the last verified custodian and opens the appropriate exception. Records must reflect the actual custody chain.

## 3. Rider and Courier oversight
The Provider verifies, approves, trains and appropriately supervises/engages assigned personnel, and restricts unauthorized access. No Rider acceptance step is implied where Zefanya uses reserved assignments. A suspended or unavailable worker may still physically hold a parcel: assign a named recovery owner, record attempts, receive the parcel with verified physical evidence before reassignment, and escalate unresolved incidents.

## 4. COD and remittance
Record the full original COD due, actual counted cash, receipt staff/account, reason/reference and any shortage/excess separately. Report and investigate discrepancies. Preserve individual explanations and authorized Admin escalation. **No automatic Rider deduction or presumption of misconduct.** Logistics-to-platform remittance and origin-provider shipping shares remain separate auditable obligations. An external payment is recorded as paid only when verified.

## 5. Delivery proof, returns and loss
Authorized delivery requires the agreed proof photo, recipient acknowledgement and exact COD confirmation when applicable; no Buyer delivery OTP is required. Failed attempts do not alone authorize Seller return. Return pickups and inter-provider reverse routes require verified custody. Providers promptly investigate missing/damaged parcels; financial responsibility depends on facts, law and approved agreements—not merely a software assignment.

## 6. Hub delay accountability
The operational triggers are 24-hour internal warning, 48-hour escalation and Buyer notice, five-day mandatory review, and seven-day assessment of cancellation/refund/compensation. These are review and escalation steps, **not automatic refunds, automatic physical dispatch, or legal liability conclusions**. Maintain a named owner, timestamp and recorded decision. Verified movement—not a mere acknowledgement—ends the hub wait.

## 7. Fees, earnings and transport
Shipping allocations and worker payments use authorized rate snapshots and provider agreements. A route may be operationally valid without guaranteed profitability. Trip profitability warning is not an approval rule; automatic price increases or subsidy decisions require explicit approved policy. Provider employment, wage and statutory duties cannot be waived through Zefanya platform text.

## 8. Recipient privacy and evidence security
Limit Buyer addresses/contact to authorized fulfillment; Couriers should see only necessary manifests/hub details. Photos, ID records and messages remain private. Implement lawful data-sharing/processing arrangements and prompt privacy incident reporting. Provider must not send customer data to unrelated parties.

## 9. Suspension, termination and reconciliation
On suspension/termination, reconcile parcels, verified custody, COD, outstanding Provider-to-platform obligations, worker payment obligations, privacy access and unresolved disputes. Preserve lawful evidence and do not erase financial records through account closure.

## 10. Liability and lawful remedies
Parties remain accountable under applicable contracts and Philippine law. Platform terms cannot defeat consumer protections or automatically impose wage deductions. Disputed responsibility requires evidenced review and appropriate remedies.
