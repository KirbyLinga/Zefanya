# Zefanya Buyer Protection, Return, Refund and Delivery Dispute Policy

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE] | **Support:** [INSERT MONITORED CHANNEL]

## 1. Coverage and legal rights
This policy explains ordinary complaint, physical return, non-receipt and refund procedures. Nothing restricts non-waivable consumer rights, applicable product warranties, or remedies for defects, loss without the Buyer's fault, nonconformity, and other qualifying issues. The original Seller remains responsible for product obligations, with Zefanya's marketplace responsibilities determined under applicable law.

## 2. Initial online reporting windows
- **Parcel Not Received:** Buyer may open an ordinary in-app dispute within **seven calendar days after recorded delivery**, including if the order has already been marked Completed. Logistics investigates; Admin handles escalations. Opening a case does **not** automatically authorize a refund or prove misconduct.
- **Wrong, damaged, defective or incomplete goods:** Buyer may open an ordinary in-app claim within **seven calendar days of recorded delivery**, even following manual or automatic completion. Seller/product return review and Admin escalation apply.
- **After the ordinary window:** Buyer can contact support for eligible warranty, legal and otherwise justified claims. The seven-day UI window **does not extinguish statutory consumer rights**.

## 3. Change of mind and exclusions
A change of preference alone does not ordinarily require a refund for conforming goods, unless law or a disclosed Seller voluntary policy provides otherwise. No blanket “No Return, No Exchange” clause applies to defective or nonconforming goods. Claims may be reviewed for misuse or customer-caused damage, but decisions must be evidence-based.

## 4. Filing and review
Submit order reference, reason, concise explanation and any reasonably available photos or other evidence via the existing order action or support. An unboxing video is not an unconditional prerequisite to lawful remedies. Sellers assess goods-related issues; Logistics examines delivery/custody issues; Admin reviews escalations. The process must display ownership, status and a follow-up path. A report is not an automatic financial reversal or accusation.

## 5. Physical reverse logistics
Authorized return transport must follow verified custody and Seller physical receipt procedures. For qualifying returns requiring free transport under law, costs are not passed to Buyers. A return-approved state does not mean physical receipt, stock restoration or money paid; those steps are independently verified. Goods should be returned where reasonably and lawfully required, unless loss or another valid circumstance prevents it.

## 6. Refund calculation and payment
The remedy may be repair, replacement, full or proportionate refund, or another remedy according to law and the circumstances. Appropriate shipping costs must be considered where required. A refund cannot be recorded as *paid* without verified payment; COD never collected cannot be described as refunded. The actual payment method, responsible payer, amount, reference and processing status must be documented. Zefanya must not claim instant wallet/card refunds without functioning payment integration. If a Seller has already been paid, an authorized debt/recovery entry must not silently rewrite history.

## 7. Delivery failures and payment disputes
Failed delivery is distinct from approved return; Logistics decides reattempt, station receipt and reverse route. An incident investigation cannot invent physical custody. Cash discrepancies are investigated with evidence and an audit trail. Non-receipt claims use existing photo/acknowledgement evidence while acknowledging that a photo alone is not independent proof of receipt.

## 8. Completion, dispute holds and escalation
Order completion is a financial/operational milestone, not a waiver. An unresolved eligible case may hold only the related unsettled payment as authorized; paid settlements remain historically intact with separate adjustments if necessary. Internal complaints are logged with submission date; under RA 11967, the internal mechanism is deemed exhausted if a complaint remains unresolved after **seven calendar days from filing**. That rule is separate from the initial reporting windows.

## 9. False complaints and fair process
Deliberately fabricated documents or claims may result in proportionate, documented action; filing a legitimate complaint is not an offense. Buyers may request support review and pursue appropriate legal remedies.
