## 1. Who we are and scope
Zefanya connects Buyers and independent Sellers and coordinates fulfillment through participating Logistics Providers, Delivery Riders and Transport Couriers. This notice covers Zefanya's website, mobile apps, and related support channels, including account administrators and parcel recipients who may not have Zefanya accounts. Participating Sellers or Logistics Providers may also act as independent personal information controllers for their own lawful processing, subject to their own notices and appropriate agreements.

## 2. Information processed by role
| Role | Categories used when necessary |
|---|---|
| Buyer / recipient | Name, email, phone, delivery address, product/order details, address snapshot, order and complaint history, in-app communications, recipient acknowledgement, delivery evidence, COD and refund records |
| Seller | Store and representative details, verification documents when required, listings, inventory, order fulfillment, customer communications, payouts, return records, debts |
| Logistics Provider | Company identity and approval, provincial/barangay coverage, hubs, authorized representatives, parcel and manifest events, remittances, incidents, assigned workers |
| Rider | Verified identity, contact, station/provider affiliation, eligibility, availability, assigned jobs, proof, recipient acknowledgement, COD liabilities, earnings and relevant incidents |
| Transport Courier | Identity, affiliation, assigned trips, vehicles/routes if necessary, manifests, verified receipts, trip pay and incidents |
| Admin / staff | Role permissions, decisions, access/audit logs, investigation notes, security events |

We may also process device and network information, session identifiers, app diagnostics, abuse and security logs. We do **not** treat the entire personal information above as accessible to every role. We do not describe precise continuous GPS tracking unless separately implemented, disclosed, and lawfully authorized.

## 3. Purposes and lawful bases
We process data for account creation, authentication, verification, marketplace transactions, seller operations, parcel delivery and custody, COD reconciliation, payouts, returns, fraud prevention, user support, consumer complaints, auditing, and legal compliance. We identify the appropriate legal basis for each purpose, including contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests where permitted, or consent when required. Consent is not presumed to be the lawful basis for all essential processing. Optional promotional messages require separate choices where applicable.

## 4. Who sees which information
Buyers see their own transactions; Sellers see their own orders and fulfillment-relevant customer details; Logistics users see information relating to their authorized provider and operational assignments; Riders see necessary assigned stop and collection details; Couriers see trip/manifests and necessary station data, not unrestricted Buyer profiles; Admin access is limited by assigned duties. Access is withdrawn when no longer needed. Sharing is based on transaction necessity and documented data-sharing/processing roles. We do not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Delivery proof and messaging
A delivery may include a photograph, recipient name or relationship, parcel reference, time, and payment confirmation. Personnel must avoid photographing IDs, unrelated people, home interiors, or other unnecessary private material. Proof is accessed only for legitimate fulfillment, dispute investigation, or lawful reasons; it must not be posted to social media or reused for marketing. Marketplace messages may be used to support orders, investigate complaints, and address abuse subject to access controls. Any optional location permissions must be separately disclosed and limited to a genuine feature.

## 6. Verification and high-risk records
Identity documents, bank/payment references, private addresses, delivery evidence, and investigatory information receive heightened access and retention controls. Passwords and security secrets are not requested in messages. Account security OTP, where used, remains distinct from the OTP-free parcel delivery procedure.

## 7. Service providers and international transfers
We may engage hosting, cloud storage, email, push notification, analytics, fraud-prevention and technical service providers **only as actually used and identified in our maintained vendor register**. We document contractual safeguards and cross-border transfers if service providers process data outside the Philippines. This notice is not a statement that a particular provider, analytics SDK, ad network, or continuous tracking service is currently installed.

## 8. Protection and security incidents
We use risk-appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards including authentication, restricted access, transport security, logging, and secure handling of evidence. No online system is risk-free. Suspected incidents are investigated; where a breach is legally notifiable, we notify the National Privacy Commission and affected individuals within the applicable legal timeline, including the relevant 72-hour requirement.

## 9. Retention and deletion
We retain each category only as long as necessary for its purpose, applicable law, audit/financial obligations, or legitimate claims. Exact retention periods are maintained in an approved **internal retention schedule**, covering IDs, chats, proof images, access logs, COD, settlements and disputes. Once no longer needed, information is securely deleted, anonymized or disposed of. Account closure does not automatically erase records legally needed for unsettled orders, financial obligations or claims. We do not promise instant or universal deletion.

## 11. Children and third-party recipients
Account eligibility and contracting capacity follow applicable law; Riders and Couriers must meet lawful work eligibility. Where an order names another recipient, the Buyer should provide only details necessary for delivery and should inform that recipient where reasonably possible. We process recipient information only for fulfillment and legitimate related purposes.
